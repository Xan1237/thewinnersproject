Winnersbench compares models that drive a simulated Unitree G1 through short tasks. Every episode is a real attempt recorded by the arena; results stream into this page as they arrive.

The task

Walk to the armchair or the lectern and stop within 1.2 m, or walk to the table, pick up the coffee cup and hold it.

Each model attempts the same tasks with the same 60-step budget per episode. The sim records the robot's full physics state and every decision, with its latency and cost.

How an episode is scored

OutcomeScoreRule
Failed0The task was not completed before the episode ended.
Succeeded60–10060 for completing the task, plus up to 40 for finishing early: 40 × (1 − steps / 60).

A model's score is the mean over its episodes, so a dot starts rough and settles as evidence accumulates. Pale dots have fewer than six episodes. Success is the share of completed episodes.

Cost

Cost is the provider spend recorded on every step (orchestrator and decision calls), averaged per episode and plotted on a log scale. Scripted baselines call no API and sit in the free column; they show what the robot manages with hand-written logic.

Live data

  • Replay
    Recorded episodes from input_layer/runs are replayed one at a time, in an interleaved order so every model advances together. The loop restarts after a short pause.
  • Live
    When new episodes land in a run file, the board settles to the full recorded history and streams only the new results.
  • Arena
    When the sim server is running, robots that are walking pulse on the plot and training progress of the free policy appears under it.

This is a local benchmark of one setup, not a general ranking of model capability.
